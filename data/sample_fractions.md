# Fractions: Parts of a Whole

## 1. What is a fraction?

A **fraction** describes part of a whole. In the fraction 3/4, the bottom number
is the **denominator**. It tells us how many equal parts the whole is divided into.
The top number is the **numerator**. It tells us how many of those parts we have.

Example: A roti is cut into 4 equal pieces and you eat 3. You ate 3/4 of the roti.

The parts must be equal. If a roti is torn into 4 pieces of different sizes,
one piece is not 1/4.

## 2. Equivalent fractions

Two fractions are **equivalent** when they name the same amount.
1/2, 2/4 and 4/8 are all equivalent.

To make an equivalent fraction, multiply (or divide) the numerator and the
denominator by the same non-zero number:

- 1/2 = (1 × 3)/(2 × 3) = 3/6
- 6/9 = (6 ÷ 3)/(9 ÷ 3) = 2/3

Dividing until no common factor is left is called **simplifying**. 2/3 is the
simplest form of 6/9.

## 3. Comparing fractions

If two fractions have the **same denominator**, the one with the larger
numerator is larger: 5/8 > 3/8.

If the denominators are different, first rewrite both fractions with a
**common denominator**, then compare the numerators.

Example: compare 2/3 and 3/4. A common denominator is 12.
2/3 = 8/12 and 3/4 = 9/12, so 3/4 is larger.

## 4. Adding fractions with the same denominator

When the denominators are the same, add the numerators and keep the denominator:

- 2/7 + 3/7 = 5/7

A common mistake is to add the denominators too. 2/7 + 3/7 is **not** 5/14.
The size of each part (sevenths) does not change when you add.

## 5. Adding fractions with different denominators

You can only add parts of the same size. To add fractions with different
denominators:

1. Find a common denominator. The **least common denominator (LCD)** is the
   smallest number both denominators divide into.
2. Rewrite each fraction as an equivalent fraction with that denominator.
3. Add the numerators and keep the common denominator.
4. Simplify if possible.

Example: 1/4 + 1/6

- The LCD of 4 and 6 is 12.
- 1/4 = 3/12 and 1/6 = 2/12.
- 3/12 + 2/12 = 5/12.

Example: 1/2 + 1/3 = 3/6 + 2/6 = 5/6.
