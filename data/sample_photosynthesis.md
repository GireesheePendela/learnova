# How Plants Make Their Food: Photosynthesis

## 1. What plants need

Animals eat food, but green plants make their own food. To do this, a plant needs
three things: **sunlight**, **water** and **carbon dioxide**, a gas in the air.

Example: A rice plant in a field gets sunlight from the sky, water from the wet soil,
and carbon dioxide from the air around its leaves.

## 2. Parts of a plant and their jobs

- The **roots** hold the plant in the soil and take in water and minerals.
- The **stem** carries water from the roots up to the leaves, and carries food
  from the leaves to the rest of the plant.
- The **leaves** are where the plant makes its food. Tiny openings on the leaves,
  called **stomata**, let carbon dioxide in and oxygen out.

## 3. Chlorophyll

Leaves are green because they contain **chlorophyll**. Chlorophyll is a green
substance that captures the energy in sunlight. Without chlorophyll, a plant
cannot use sunlight to make food.

A leaf kept in a dark room for a few days turns pale, because it cannot make food.

## 4. Photosynthesis

**Photosynthesis** is the process plants use to make food. In the leaves, the plant
uses the energy of sunlight, captured by chlorophyll, to turn water and carbon
dioxide into **glucose** (a sugar) and **oxygen**.

In words:

carbon dioxide + water → (sunlight, chlorophyll) → glucose + oxygen

The plant uses the glucose for energy and to grow. It releases the oxygen into the air.

A common mistake is to think plants get their food from the soil. The soil gives
water and minerals, but the food (glucose) is made in the leaves.

## 5. Why photosynthesis matters

- **Oxygen:** The oxygen that people and animals breathe comes from photosynthesis.
- **Food chains:** Plants are **producers**. A goat eats grass, and a person may eat
  the goat or drink its milk. Every food chain starts with a plant that made food
  by photosynthesis.
- **Less carbon dioxide:** Plants take carbon dioxide out of the air. Cutting down
  many trees leaves more carbon dioxide in the air.

## Summary

Plants need sunlight, water and carbon dioxide. Roots take in water, the stem carries
it, and the leaves make food. Chlorophyll captures sunlight. Photosynthesis turns
carbon dioxide and water into glucose and oxygen, which gives us the air we breathe
and starts every food chain.
